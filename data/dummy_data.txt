1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
============================================================

TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional technology company providing software development, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and consulting services.

The company employs full-time employees, part-time employees, interns, contractors, and temporary workers.

The purpose of this handbook is to define workplace rules, employee benefits, responsibilities, leave policies, attendance requirements, compensation practices, and employee conduct standards.

============================================================
2. EMPLOYMENT TYPES
============================================================

2.1 Full-Time Employee

A full-time employee normally works 40 hours per week.

Full-time employees are eligible for benefits according to their employment agreement and applicable company policies.

2.2 Part-Time Employee

A part-time employee works fewer than 40 hours per week.

Benefits may differ from full-time employees depending on the employee's contract.

2.3 Intern

Interns are individuals hired for a temporary learning and work experience program.

Interns:
- Must follow company policies.
- Must maintain assigned working hours.
- May receive a stipend according to their internship agreement.
- Are not automatically eligible for all full-time employee benefits.

2.4 Contractor

Contractors provide services under a contractual agreement.

Contractors must comply with security, confidentiality, workplace safety, and acceptable-use policies.

2.5 Temporary Employee

Temporary employees are hired for a predefined period or specific project.

Their employment automatically ends according to the terms of their contract unless extended.

3. RECRUITMENT AND HIRING POLICY
============================================================

TechNova follows a structured hiring process.

Typical hiring stages are:
1. Application
2. Resume screening
3. Recruiter discussion
4. Technical/functional interview
5. Managerial interview
6. HR interview
7. Background verification
8. Offer
9. Documentation
10. Joining

3.1 Equal Opportunity

Employment decisions should be based on job-related qualifications, skills, experience, and business requirements.

Selected candidates may be required to provide information necessary for background verification.

3.3 Employee Referral

Employees may refer candidates for open positions.

The referring employee must not misrepresent the candidate's qualifications.

89. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

Q1. How many hours does a full-time employee normally work?

A full-time employee normally works 40 hours per week, subject to their employment agreement and team schedule.

Q2. Can I work from home whenever I want?

Not necessarily. Remote work depends on role eligibility, business requirements, and applicable approval.

Q3. Who approves leave?

Normally the employee's manager approves leave, while HR maintains the official leave records.

Q4. Can I take leave during probation?

Leave eligibility during probation depends on the applicable employment terms and law.

Q5. What happens if an employee repeatedly arrives late?

Repeated lateness may lead to counseling, warnings, an improvement plan, or further disciplinary action depending on the circumstances.

Q6. Can I use ChatGPT with company data?

Only approved AI tools and approved use cases should be used for company information. Confidential information must not be entered into unapproved AI systems.

Q7. What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?

Do not click suspicious links or attachments. Report the message through the company's security reporting process.

Q8. Can I work for another company?

Outside employment may require disclosure and must not create conflicts of interest or interfere with your responsibilities.

Q9. What happens after resignation?

The employee normally serves the applicable notice period, completes knowledge transfer, returns company property, and completes the exit process.

Q10. Who should I contact for salary issues?

Employees should contact the HR/payroll team through the designated employee support channel.
